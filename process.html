OUR APPROACH

Assess. Analyze. Implement. Sustain.

A consistent, evidence-based consulting model that turns operational challenges into prioritized actions and measurable improvements.

01

Assess

Review current performance, gather baseline data, interview stakeholders, and identify risks and opportunities.

02

Analyze

Determine root causes, quantify business impact, prioritize initiatives, and agree on a measurable action plan.

03

Implement

Work with client teams to implement corrective actions, improve processes, develop capabilities, and track progress.

04

Sustain

Monitor KPIs, verify effectiveness, standardize improvements, and establish ownership to maintain results.

ENGAGEMENT MODELS

Choose the right starting point

Engagement scope and timing are finalized after discussing the facility, problem statement, data availability, and desired outcomes.

DIAGNOSE

Diagnostic Assessment

Focused review, findings, and a prioritized action plan.

Typical planning range: 1–2 weeks

DELIVER

Improvement Project

A defined project with implementation support and measurable objectives.

Typical planning range: 4–12 weeks

PARTNER

Ongoing Partnership

Recurring support for quality systems, supplier quality, or operational improvement.

Monthly or quarterly

These are indicative planning durations, not fixed commitments. Actual timing depends on facility size, scope, data availability, and project complexity.

MEASUREMENT & ACCOUNTABILITY

Make progress visible. Verify what lasts.

Each project should establish a baseline, define success measures, assign action owners, and agree on how results will be verified. The exact measures depend on the problem and the client’s data.

Examples include defect and complaint trends, audit closure, supplier rejection rates, first-pass yield, scrap and rework, throughput, cycle time, and finance-validated savings.
