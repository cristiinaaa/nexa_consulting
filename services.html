OUR SERVICES

Integrated support for manufacturing performance.

Four connected service lines help manufacturers strengthen quality, develop suppliers, reduce costs, and improve process performance.

01 / QUALITY FOUNDATION

Quality Systems Development & Improvement

Establish, improve, and sustain effective Quality Management Systems that support customer requirements, applicable standards, and business objectives.

Scope of support

  • QMS assessment and gap analysis against ISO 9001 and applicable customer requirements
  • Policies, objectives, procedures, process maps, work instructions, and record controls
  • Nonconformance, corrective action, change management, and risk-based thinking
  • Risk-based internal audit programs, audit execution, and auditor training
  • Customer and external audit readiness, corrective-action closure, and effectiveness checks
  • Quality KPIs, dashboards, trend analysis, and management review support

Typical deliverables

  • QMS gap assessment and risk-ranked roadmap
  • Updated documentation and process maps
  • Internal audit schedule, checklists, and reports
  • KPI dashboard and management review package
  • Corrective-action tracking and effectiveness verification

Measures of success

Customer complaints, internal defects, audit findings and closure, corrective-action timeliness, scrap, rework, and process compliance.

Best suited for: Manufacturers with inconsistent quality processes, recurring audit findings, growing customer requirements, or plans to implement or improve ISO 9001.

02 / SUPPLY CHAIN

Supplier Development & Supplier Audits

Improve supplier quality, delivery reliability, process capability, and responsiveness through evidence-based assessment and focused improvement.

Scope of support

  • Supplier quality, delivery, responsiveness, and service performance assessments
  • Supplier scorecards, risk classifications, and prioritization of critical suppliers
  • On-site or remote supplier audits covering QMS, production controls, inspection, traceability, and document control
  • Review of calibration, nonconformance handling, containment, and corrective-action effectiveness
  • Root cause and 8D, 5 Whys, and Fishbone problem-solving support
  • Supplier coaching, improvement plans, follow-up assessments, and effectiveness checks

Typical deliverables

  • Supplier audit checklist and audit report
  • Supplier risk assessment and performance scorecard
  • Corrective-action register and 8D review
  • Supplier development plan with milestones
  • Follow-up audit and improvement verification report

Measures of success

Supplier defects and incoming rejections, on-time delivery, repeat nonconformances, production disruptions, and corrective-action effectiveness.

Best suited for: Manufacturers dealing with recurring incoming inspection failures, supplier delivery issues, or limited resources for supplier development.

Audit scope is tailored to the client’s requirements. Specialized standards require appropriate sector knowledge and qualifications.

03 / FINANCIAL IMPACT

Cost Reduction

Identify, quantify, and implement savings opportunities while protecting product quality, customer satisfaction, and process reliability.

Scope of support

  • Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ) analysis for scrap, rework, defects, returns, warranty, and complaints
  • Material usage, yield, labor utilization, waste, cycle time, throughput, and downtime analysis
  • Supplier-related costs such as sorting, rework, returns, and expedited freight
  • Cross-functional project charters, root-cause analysis, implementation, and follow-through
  • Financial baselines, savings calculations, measurement periods, and sustainment controls

Typical deliverables

  • COPQ assessment and cost-reduction opportunity register
  • Prioritized savings project portfolio and project charters
  • Verified savings calculations and financial tracking
  • Implementation plans and sustainment controls

Measures of success

Scrap and rework cost, COPQ, first-pass yield, expedited freight, supplier failure costs, conversion cost per unit, and validated savings.

Best suited for: Manufacturers facing margin pressure, excessive scrap, high rework costs, low yields, or recurring poor-quality costs.

Savings should be clearly classified as identified, implemented, or finance-validated. Financial validation depends on agreed baselines and measurement periods.

04 / OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE

Process & Continuous Improvement

Improve process efficiency, consistency, productivity, and flow using Lean principles, Six Sigma methods, structured problem solving, and data.

Scope of support

  • Current-state process mapping and bottleneck assessment
  • Cycle time, throughput, capacity, changeover, and workflow analysis
  • Lean methods including 5S, visual management, Value Stream Mapping, Kaizen, and standard work
  • DMAIC projects, SPC, process capability, Pareto analysis, Fishbone, and 5 Whys
  • Daily performance reviews, visual boards, KPI development, and team coaching
  • Control plans, standardization, and sustainment verification

Typical deliverables

  • Current-state assessment and process maps
  • Value Stream Maps and prioritized project portfolio
  • Lean/Six Sigma project charters and root-cause reports
  • KPI dashboards and visual management tools
  • Standard work, control plans, and sustainment plans

Measures of success

Throughput, productivity, cycle and changeover time, first-pass yield, process capability, variation, downtime, delivery, and sustained savings.

Best suited for: Manufacturers with bottlenecks, long cycle times, excessive waste, recurring process problems, or a need for a stronger continuous improvement culture.

Not just assessment. Practical implementation and follow-through.

Each engagement is scoped around your priorities, baseline data, and desired business outcomes.
